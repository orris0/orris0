Our Mission

Family Media Network centers survivors of assaults and abuse in New York City Safe Haven and transitional shelters—especially disabled and undocumented people—seeking safety, help, accountability, and pathways to support.

This is a community advocacy resource. It is not affiliated with NYC agencies or shelter operators.

Context on the Shelters

Public reporting and city data have documented elevated violence and serious incidents in parts of the NYC shelter system, including Safe Havens and BRC-operated sites. Individual experiences vary.

  • The Kelly / XX Street Kelly Safe Haven
  • The Andrews Safe Haven (Breaking Ground)
  • Traveler’s / Travellers Safe Haven
  • Breaking Ground programs
  • BRC transitional and Safe Haven programs (including 25th Street area)

Know Your Rights

This is not legal advice. Always consult an independent advocate or attorney.

  • Right to shelter – Generally available in NYC. Immigration status does not bar emergency shelter access.
  • Safety & complaints – Right to a safe environment and to file complaints without retaliation.
  • Disability accommodations – Reasonable accommodations must be provided. Request them from staff.
  • Gender identity – Placement consistent with gender identity is protected.
  • Language access – Right to interpreters for key interactions.
