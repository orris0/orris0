### Know Your Rights: NYC Safe Haven & Transitional Shelter Survival Hub This central database is maintained by the **Family Media Network** to support, protect, and legally arm survivors, disabled neighbors, and undocumented community members navigating transitional housing matrix systems. We compile real-time tracking metrics, incident chronologies, and safety parameters across **The Kelly**, **The Andrews**, **The Travellers Hotel**, **Breaking Ground Sites**, and the **BRC Transitional Shelter on 25th Street**.  ### 1. Zero Tolerance for Physical Abuse and Operational Assaults * **The Legal Mandate:** Under New York City municipal directives and New York State safety rules, every resident retains an absolute right to safe emergency housing. This right is explicitly broken by any form of physical violence, verbal degradation, sexual harassment, or physical intimidation perpetrated by facility staff or other occupants. * **Evidence Collection Guidelines:** If you experience or observe an assault, write down the date, exact time, physical descriptions or names of involved personnel or residents, and any intake or shift logs. Securely store physical or digital copies of medical treatment records or police reports outside of the shelter premises. ### 2. Enforceable Civil Rights for Disabled Residents (ADA Protections) * **Reasonable Accommodations:** Under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), transitional shelters and municipal safe havens (including the BRC 25th Street location) are legally obligated to modify environments for residents with physical, intellectual, or psychological challenges. * **Vital Infrastructure:** This standard protects your access to functional wheelchair entryways, reliable electrical feeds for life-sustaining medical machinery, and customized service timelines. Denial of these structural assets is a direct violation of civil rights law. ### 3. Absolute Privacy Barriers for Undocumented Families * **Sanctuary City Compliance:** New York City operations state that access to emergency shelter services and temporary transitional housing can **never** be withheld, restricted, or conditioned on your immigration status or country of citizenship. * **Information Lock:** Shelter management teams and private contractors are legally blocked from sharing your identifying files or personal paperwork with federal immigration enforcement (ICE) unless handed an explicit judicial court warrant. Threatening deportation to prevent you from filing safety logs is strictly illegal. ### 4. Compensation Pipelines & Protection from Retaliation * **Anti-Retaliation Guardrails:** It is illegal for shelter operators to alter your bed placement, change your tier assignment, transfer you to a different facility, or restrict your access to food because you filed a safety complaint or spoke with a legal team. * **Building a Compensation Case:** Independent legal defense groups require chronologically organized evidence to aggressively pursue financial recovery for survivors. Maintain complete backups of your intake papers, restriction notices, and written timelines to prove patterns of systemic municipal neglect.
