Frequently Asked Questions

Information on anonymity, security protocols, and documentation usage.

Are survivor testimony submissions anonymous?

Yes. All survivor testimonies can be submitted anonymously with no mandatory email or personal identifying metadata logged on our servers.

How is documented facility evidence utilized?

Compiled testimonies and incident logs serve as official evidence for advocacy campaigns, policy proposals, and public oversight investigations with DHS representatives.

Who manages the Family Media Network?

The network is privately maintained by verified family administrators while offering public access options for community advocacy and legal support resources.
