Resident Rights & Emergency Legal Guides

Know your constitutional and municipal protections in NYC shelter and safe haven systems.

Disability & ADA Protections

Under Local Law and federal ADA mandates, shelters must provide reasonable accommodations. If an elevator is broken or medical equipment access is denied, facilities are legally required to provide alternative accessible placements immediately.

Key Claim: "I am requesting a Reasonable Accommodation under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act."

Undocumented Resident Protections

NYC is a Right to Shelter city. Shelter services and safe havens cannot deny services based on immigration status, nor can staff threaten immigration enforcement.

NYC Executive Order 41: City agencies and contracted providers will not inquire about immigration status unless strictly mandated.

Language Access Rights

Facilities are required to provide free interpretation and translated critical documents. You do not need to bring your own translator or rely on family members.

Request: "I require language translation services in my primary language under Local Law 30."

Protection Against Retaliation

Filing a complaint with the OTDA (NYS Office of Temporary and Disability Assistance) or calling 311/DHS Ombudsman is a protected action. Retaliatory transfers or bed loss are illegal.

NYS OTDA Hotline: 1-800-342-3009 | NYC DHS Ombudsman: 800-994-6494
